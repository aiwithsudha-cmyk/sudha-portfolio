How this site was made.

A short note on the type, colour and tools behind it.

Type
Fraunces for headings and IBM Plex Sans for everything else, both served through Google Fonts.
Colour
Four palettes, each with a light and a dark mode, on a warm base. Open the Theme menu in the header to switch. Your choice is remembered, and the site follows your device's light or dark setting until you pick.
  • Berry & olive
  • Salmon, with a black and white stripe
  • Cobalt & marigold
  • Lagoon & rose
Built with
Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no framework. Edited in VS Code, kept on GitHub and hosted on Netlify.
Made with
Planned and drafted in conversation with Claude, from the page structure and palettes to the code.
Small details
A scroll progress bar at the top and a percentage in the footer. The footer clock shows Mumbai time. Your theme choice is stored in your browser, and nowhere else.
Inspired by
Serg Zorin, Sierra Hopkins, Emmi Wu, Shruthi Aragonda and Charlsy Yang.
